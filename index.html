Track your NSW market permits and COI endorsements across councils — in one place.

For stallholders juggling more than one LGA and more than one organiser upload. Not advice. Not an organiser tool.

The problem

If you sell food at markets across NSW, you often deal with separate council processes — different portals, different fees, different expiry rules (financial-year end in one place, twelve-month approvals in another, event-linked renewals elsewhere). On top of that, some councils ask to be named as an interested party on your certificate of currency, and every market organiser wants their own copy of insurance and approvals uploaded again.

Organiser portals help them chase your documents for that one market. They do not keep your multi-council calendar or remind you which council still needs to appear on the COI after a policy renewal. Spreadsheets and email folders are the usual workaround. That is the admin mess this waitlist is about — not how to cook safely, and not which licence you should hold.

What it will NOT do

  • Not food-safety advice (no stall set-up, temperature, or Food Standards guidance).
  • Not which permit to get (no principal vs recognition, no “you need this licence”).
  • Not insurance advice (no limits, products, brokers, or endorsement wording to buy).
  • Not an organiser / market operating system.
  • Not legal advice.

If you need those things, talk to your council, insurer/broker, or a qualified adviser — not this page.

What it will do (planned)

  • Help you monitor renewals and expiries across the NSW LGAs and policies you already track.
  • Remind you when a COI needs a named council (interested party) checked off — as a checklist of parties you recorded, not a recommendation of cover.
  • Help you track which organiser (and which council portal) got which upload after a renewal.

Early version: calendar + checklists + reminders. Built for the stallholder, not the market operator.

FAQ

Is this for market organisers?
No. This is for stallholders who work across multiple councils and organisers.

Will it tell me which council permit I need?
No. You decide that with the relevant council. We only help track dates and uploads you already know about.

Will it tell me what insurance to buy?
No. Your insurer or broker handles cover.

Is FoodTrader enough?
FoodTrader is Victoria’s state portal. NSW still runs per-council processes for many temporary and mobile food paths.

Is there a product today?
Not yet. This page measures whether enough stallholders care about the admin glue to justify building anything. No payment. No app download.
